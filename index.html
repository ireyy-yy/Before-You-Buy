About Before You Buy

Buying land is a major decision, but understanding the land and the information surrounding it can be difficult when data is scattered across government portals, maps, and technical records. Before You Buy makes this process simpler by helping citizens understand the area they are considering before making a purchase.

Using ISRO Bhuvan satellite imagery and district-level land-use data, the app turns complex land information into visual maps, simple explanations, and practical buyer-oriented insights. It then guides users through the key things they should independently verify before purchasing, including survey or plot numbers, ownership details, 7/12 extracts or Property Cards, Ferfar/mutation records, NA or conversion status, and zoning or planning restrictions.

The app does not verify ownership or certify whether a property is legally safe to purchase. Instead, it connects users to the relevant official government records and portals, explaining what each record is for and what they should look for.

Our goal is simple: help people ask the right questions, understand the information available to them, and make more informed land-purchase decisions without relying entirely on agents or intermediaries.
